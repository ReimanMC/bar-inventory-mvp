LA RAMONA INVENTORY — VALIDATION V0.5.10

Scope
-----
Correction of live DAILY Opening/Closing workflow. No change to Supabase secrets, bucket, weekly inventory tables, POS formulas or dashboard KPIs.

Validated scenarios
-------------------
1. Fresh system: Opening is available immediately for the current local date.
2. Opening completed on Sep 17; current date Sep 20: Closing remains enabled and linked to Sep 17.
3. Partial Closing across days: Closing remains the only live action until required products are complete.
4. Completed Closing: a new Opening becomes available immediately; there is no WAIT_NEXT_OPENING / next-calendar-day gate.
5. Same-day reopen: the new shift uses the previous Closing session ID as a boundary, so prior shift counts are not reused as progress.
6. Historical paper transcriptions tagged TRANSCRIPCIÓN HISTÓRICA POR CONTINGENCIA do not block the live workflow.
7. Product-set freeze: a shift started with products {1,2,3} stays complete even if products 4 and 5 are later added to the daily catalog.
8. Legacy compatibility: if a legacy Closing had already started, its Opening product set defines the expected products instead of later catalog additions.
9. Opening reference now includes the latest Closing on or before the opening date, supporting after-midnight/variable schedule operations.
10. Python compilation: PASS.

Operational rule
----------------
Opening and Closing are controlled by shift state, not clock/calendar boundaries. The exact real timestamp remains recorded for audit. The business date of the Closing remains the Opening business date for reconciliation.

PASS for all targeted workflow tests.
